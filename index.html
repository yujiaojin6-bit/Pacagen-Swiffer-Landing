Questions, answered

Do I need a new mop?

No. MAX is a refill for the Swiffer Sweeper Mop you already have. That is the point — no new system.

How is $1.25 per session calculated?

$14.99 ÷ 12 cloths = $1.25 per cloth, with 1 MAX cloth per cleaning session. The old cloth costs about $5.00 per session (10–12 pads).

What exactly is the one improvement?

One thing only: a longer-lasting MAX cloth that stays wet longer and covers more floor per cloth — 20X thicker, 10X longer lasting wetness, as shown on the pack. Same mop, same refill habit.

How many cloths per clean?

About 10–12 of the old thin cloths for one clean — versus 1 MAX cloth per clean.

When will my order ship? How much is shipping?

This is a concept mockup, so there is no real shipping. In the demo, shipping is free on your first order (concept).
