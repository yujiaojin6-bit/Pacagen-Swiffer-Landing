Product Information

Mop & Go MAX is a concept for thicker, disposable, pre-moistened wet cloth refills. The design aims to retain moisture longer, clean more floor per cloth, and reduce repeated interruptions during mopping. It is intended to upgrade the Swiffer Sweeper cleaning routine without replacing the mop.

12 Mop & Go MAX disposable wet cloth refills.

Mop and handle not included.

Proposed retail price: $14.99.

Mop & Go MAX is designed as a disposable wet cloth refill for the Swiffer Sweeper flat mop. It is intended to attach using the existing Sweeper cloth-gripping system, without requiring a new mop or additional accessories.

Final fit and compatibility require prototype testing.

Moisture Retention

Thicker cloth construction designed to hold moisture longer during cleaning.

Floor Coverage

Designed to cover more floor area before a fresh refill is needed.

Fewer Interruptions

Fewer cloth changes can mean less stopping and bending down during a cleaning session.

*10× wetness and coverage figures are unverified concept targets.

Step 1 - Prepare: Remove one Mop & Go MAX cloth from the package.

Step 2 - Attach: Remove the backing from the velcro pad and firmly press the MAX cloth to your Swiffer Sweeper head, ensuring full contact for optimal adhesion.

Step 3 - Clean: Begin cleaning. MAX cloths are designed to provide extended wet-cleaning performance compared to standard cloths.

Step 4 - Replace: When the cloth becomes dry or worn, carefully peel it away and dispose of it properly. Attach a fresh cloth and continue cleaning.
