# MOP & GO MAX — Swiffer Sweeper Wet Cloth landing page (concept mockup)

A single-product landing page for Sky Jin's Pacagen Product / Marketing Associate take-home assignment (Option B: website landing page demo). Built from her pitch deck `assigb_ppt.pdf`.

## The pitch (from the deck — do not change the numbers)
- Product: Swiffer Sweeper Wet Cloth Refills, for the Swiffer Sweeper Mop.
- Pain: one thin cloth dries out quickly, covers only a small area. She changes it 6–7 times per mop; about 10 pads for just a small area.
- Cost today: ~$5.00 per session, ~10–12 cloths, 2 sessions/week, ~$520/year. Plus bending, time, frustration.
- Shared pain: +50M Sweeper wet-cloth users in the US (Swiffer); +50K bought in the past month on Amazon alone.
- Alternatives all mean a new system: Spin Mop + Bucket, Robotic Vacuum, Swiffer WetJet, Swiffer PowerMop.
- The ONE improvement: MOP & GO MAX — a longer-lasting refill cloth that stays wet longer and covers more floor per cloth. Same mop, you buy the refill anyway, get more out of every cloth.
- Price in the deck: $14.99 / 12 cloths = $1.25 per cleaning session (1 MAX cloth per session). Yearly at 2 sessions/week: $130 vs ~$520.
- Close: "Next Time When You Restock, Make It MAX."

## Theme (from the deck)
Deep dark green #0C3B2E, lime accent #B8F53D, cream #F5F7EE, white. Big bold headlines, rounded cards.

## Files

## Honesty notes (keep these)
- Amazon quotes are reviews of the ORIGINAL wet cloths, labelled as such. No fake 5-star reviews for MAX were invented — MAX is a concept, so the page says so and invites visitors to be first to try it.
- Footer discloses: concept mockup for a Pacagen interview assignment, not a real product for sale, not affiliated with / endorsed by Swiffer or P&G. Product photography and the GIF were generated for this mockup.
- All prices/numbers are the deck's own: $14.99/12, $1.25/session, ~$5/session, ~$520/year, +50M, +50K.
